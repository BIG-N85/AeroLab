Rocket Equation

Calculate ideal delta-v from the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation.

Δv = Isp × g₀ × ln(m₀ / mf)   |   g₀ = 9.80665 m/s²

Delta-V Calculator

Delta-v is the change in velocity from one state to another.

Δv = vf − vi

Projectile Motion

Ideal two-dimensional motion with a level landing surface.

vx = v₀ cos θ   vy = v₀ sin θ   y(t) = h + vy·t − ½gt²

Assumptions: no air resistance, constant g = 9.80665 m/s², and the projectile lands at ground level.

Aerodynamics: Lift Estimate

A simplified engineering model for learning and estimation.

L = 0.5 × ρ × v² × S × Cl

Simplified model only; this is not a high-fidelity aircraft simulator. ρ is air density (kg/m³), v is speed (m/s), S is reference area (m²), and Cl is coefficient of lift (unitless).

Default is a common near sea-level reference value.
